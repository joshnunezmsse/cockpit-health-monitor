# Privacy Policy for Cockpit Health Monitor

**Effective Date:** 2024-10-27

## Introduction

Welcome to Cockpit Health Monitor ("we," "our," "us"). This is an open-source application designed to help you monitor the health status of your Cockpit instances. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our Android application.

As an open-source project, our goal is transparency. The application is provided "as is," and we do not operate any servers or services that collect data from you.

## Information We Collect

Cockpit Health Monitor **does not collect any personally identifiable information (PII)**. The application's core functionality is to connect to a server URL that you provide.

The only data the application stores is:

*   **Server URL:** The URL of your Cockpit instance that you manually enter into the application. This information is stored locally on your device in the application's private storage and is never transmitted to us or any third party.

The application does not collect analytics, crash reports (other than standard Google Play reporting), or any other personal data such as your name, email address, location, or contacts.

## How We Use Your Information

The information stored by the application is used solely for its intended functionality:

*   **To Connect to Your Server:** The stored Server URL is used to make network requests directly from your device to your Cockpit instance to fetch and display health status information.
*   **To Power the App Widget:** The Server URL and the last known health status may be stored locally to keep the home screen widget up-to-date.

All data processing happens locally on your device or between your device and the server you have configured.

## Data Sharing

We **do not share your data with any third parties**. All network communication initiated by the application is exclusively between your device and the server URL you provide.

## Data Storage and Security

The Server URL you provide is stored in the application's private, sandboxed storage on your device. We rely on the standard security measures provided by the Android operating system to protect this data. You are in full control of this data and can delete it at any time.

## Your Rights

You have complete control over the data stored by this application. You can view, change, or delete the configured Server URL from within the application's settings at any time. Deleting the URL will remove it from the application's local storage.

## Children's Privacy

This application is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Effective Date" at the top. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
